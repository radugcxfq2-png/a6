About Stylora

A small independent site about dressing well without buying more.

Stylora started from an uncomfortable observation: a full wardrobe that produced four outfits. Not a lack of clothes, and not a lack of taste — a lack of compatibility. Everything went with one other thing.

That turns out to be the common case, and it is fixable with arithmetic rather than shopping. So this site covers the mechanical half of dressing well: how many pieces a wardrobe needs, which colours sit together and why, and where a hem should fall on a particular person.

What is here

Two long guides and three tools. The Capsule Wardrobe Guide covers the compatibility test, the counts per category and building around a base. The Colour Guide covers warmth, the three-colour ceiling, texture and what shop lighting hides.

The tools answer one question each, in your browser, with nothing stored and no sign-up.

How we stay independent

No brand, retailer, stylist or label can pay to be mentioned here. There are no shopping or affiliate links anywhere on the site, and nothing is sent to us. Advertising is served by a third party and has no say in what we publish or how we describe anything.

We also sell nothing, which is why this site is comfortable telling you that the most useful change is usually alterations to what you already own.

What we are not

We are not a styling service and we have not seen you. Proportions vary independently of height by several centimetres, dye lots shift how a colour reads, and body shape changes what lengths suit. Everything here is a starting point to check in a mirror, which the Disclaimer sets out in full.

Corrections

If a figure here is wrong, or does not match your experience, write to hello@stylora.com. A person reads it, corrections go to the front of the queue, and the Editorial Policy explains how we research and review what goes up.
